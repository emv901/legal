# FF 2022 — Privacy Policy

**Effective: August 18, 2026**

This policy explains what data FF 2022 collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

## Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Stored |
|------|---------|--------|
| Discord User IDs | Link Discord accounts to game profiles | Persistently in database |
| In-game nicknames | Display player identity in profiles and rankings | Persistently in database |
| Game registration codes | Authenticate and link accounts | Temporarily during login |
| Rank and match statistics | Track player progress and competitive ranking | Persistently in database |
| Outfit/skin selections | Display player customization choices | Persistently in database |
| Command interactions | Process bot commands and respond appropriately | Not stored |

## Data We Do Not Collect

- Message content (beyond slash command arguments)
- Voice or video data
- Direct messages
- Personal information such as email, real name, or IP address
- Financial or payment information
- Data from users who do not interact with the bot

## How Data Is Used

Data is used exclusively to provide account linking, profile management, ranking, and outfit features. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties. Data is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

## Data Storage and Security

Player data is stored in a PostgreSQL database and protected with reasonable technical measures. Only the bot operator has access to stored data. Login codes are used only during the authentication process and are not retained after verification.

## Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Server administrators can reset player ranks using admin commands. Full account deletion can be requested via email.

## Children's Privacy

FF 2022 does not knowingly collect data from users under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). Use of the bot requires compliance with Discord's own age requirements.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Request access to any data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
- Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority in your jurisdiction

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
