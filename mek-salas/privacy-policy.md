# MEK Salas — Privacy Policy

**Effective: August 18, 2026**

This policy explains what data MEK Salas collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

## Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Stored |
|------|---------|--------|
| Discord User IDs | Identify players in queues and match rooms | Persistently in database |
| In-game nicknames | Display player names in team lineups and results | Persistently in database |
| Match results and scores | Track game outcomes and player statistics | Persistently in database |
| Queue participation | Manage matchmaking queues | In memory during bot runtime |
| Command and button interactions | Process bot commands and respond appropriately | Not stored |

## Data We Do Not Collect

- Voice or video data
- Direct messages
- Personal information such as email, real name, or IP address
- Financial or payment information
- Data from users who do not interact with the bot

## How Data Is Used

Data is used exclusively to provide room creation, matchmaking, and result tracking. We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties. Data is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

## Data Storage and Security

Player and match data is stored in a PostgreSQL database and protected with reasonable technical measures. Only the bot operator has access to stored data. Queue data is stored in memory and cleared on bot restart.

## Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Match history and player data can be removed upon request via email.

## Children's Privacy

MEK Salas does not knowingly collect data from users under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). Use of the bot requires compliance with Discord's own age requirements.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Request access to any data we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
- Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority in your jurisdiction

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
